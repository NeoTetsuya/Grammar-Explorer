Grammar Explorer 3 Quick Reference

Past Modals of Deduction

Use Must + have + V3 for strong certainty about the past (He must have forgotten), Could/Might + have + V3 for possibility, and Should + have + V3 for regret or missed obligations.

3rd & Mixed Conditionals

3rd Conditional expresses hypothetical past conditions (If I had studied, I would have passed). Mixed conditional connects past conditions with present outcomes (If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor today).

Reported Speech Backshifting

When reporting past speech, backshift tenses: Present Simple → Past Simple, Present Perfect → Past Perfect, Will → Would. Pronouns and time signals also shift (e.g. now → then, today → that day).
