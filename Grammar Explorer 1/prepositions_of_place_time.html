Prepositions of Place

Where something is located.

At, On, and In

  • At: Specific addresses or common daily places.
    She lives at 39 Main Street.
    I am at the supermarket.
  • On: Streets, roads, avenues, and building floors.
    The cafe is on Park Street.
    My office is on the second floor.
  • In: Cities, countries, and rooms (enclosed spaces).
    They met in Paris.
    We are in Room 215.

Omission of "The"

In everyday English, do NOT use "the" before certain common locations:

Correct: at school at home at work

Prepositions of Time

When something happens.

At, In, and On

  • At: Specific clock times, midnight, or "night".
    Class starts at three-thirty.
    I sleep at night.
  • In: Years, months, seasons, and parts of the day.
    Born in 2015.
    We play tennis in the morning.
  • On: Specific dates, days of the week, and weekends.
    The party is on June 29.
    I relax on weekends.

Multiple Phrases

You can use more than one prepositional phrase to be precise:

He wakes up at five-thirty in the morning.

Spatial Descriptors

Words to describe the physical position of objects relative to one another.

In / Inside Inside an enclosed space (in the glass).
On Resting on a surface (on the book).
Next to / Near Beside / close to something.
Between In the middle of two objects.
In front of / Behind Ahead of / At the rear of something.
Over / Under Higher than / Lower than something.
Across from Facing on the opposite side (e.g., across the hall).

Time Boundaries

From... To...

Establishes the exact start and end points.

She works from nine to five-thirty.

Until

Describes an ongoing action up to a boundary time.

The bank is open until four o'clock.

Approximating Time

Use around or about before the time to be less specific.

We eat dinner at about 8:00.

Formulating Questions

Where (Place)

Verb "be" MUST go before the subject.

Where is the park?
Where the park is?
Contractions:
  • Where + is = Where's
  • Where + are = Where are (NEVER Where're)

When (Time)

Verb "be" MUST go before the subject.

When is the test?
When the test is?
Contractions:
  • When + is = When's (e.g., When's your birthday?)
