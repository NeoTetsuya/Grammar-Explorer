Unit 15: There Is / There Are & It

Unit 15 Grammar

Existence, Time, and Weather

Learn how to declare that something exists using There is / There are, and master the use of the pronoun It for time and weather conditions.

1. There Is / There Are

Used to state that something exists.

There Is

Use with Singular & Non-Count nouns.

There is a hospital.

There Are

Use with Plural count nouns.

There are two elevators.
THE LIST RULE

Agreement with Multiple Nouns

If followed by a list, the verb usually agrees with the first noun in the list.

There's a book and three pens. Verb agrees with the singular 'book'
There are some cookies and juice. Verb agrees with the plural 'cookies'

Using "No" for Negatives

"There is no..." means the exact same as "There isn't any..."

There is no homework tonight.

2. Questions & Quantities

Yes / No Questions

Reverse the order of There and the verb Be.

Question Is there an entrance?
Answer Yes, there is.
No contractions for affirmative short answers.
Conversational Tip: Use "There's one" in spoken English to avoid repeating a singular noun.
"Is there a bank? Yes, there's one nearby."

Quantity Questions

  • How much homework is there? Use with Non-Count Nouns
  • How many computers are there? Use with Plural Count Nouns

3. Using "It" (Time & Weather)

The pronoun it is the standard subject for time, dates, and weather.

Time & Dates

  • Q: What time is it?
  • Q: What day is it?
  • A: It is 2:00.
  • A: It's Monday.

Weather

  • Q: How is the weather?
  • Q: What's the weather like?
  • A: It's warm and sunny.

Crucial Rule

Do not use the pronoun it in time questions that start with What is or What's.

What is the time?
What is it the time?

4. Spelling Distinctions

There vs. They're vs. Their

There

Used for existence or to mean "in/to that place."

Example: There is a man in the boat.

They're

The contraction for "they are".

Example: They're in the park.

Their

The possessive adjective (showing ownership).

Example: Their house is on Pine Street.
