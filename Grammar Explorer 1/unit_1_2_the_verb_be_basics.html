1. The Verb "Be" & Nouns

Conjugation & Subject Agreement

Singular
  • I → am (I'm)
  • He, She, It → is (He's)
Plural (and You)
  • We, You, They → are (We're)
He is from Brazil. Singular
You are students. Plural/You

Articles: A vs. An

Use before singular count nouns based on the starting sound.

  • A doctor (Consonant sound)
  • An engineer (Vowel sound)
  • A university (Starts with 'y' sound)
  • An hour (Silent 'h')

2. Possessives & Questions

Showing Ownership

Possessive Adjectives

my, your, his, her, its, our, their

This is her job.
Possessive Nouns
  • Singular: Add 's (The baby's room)
  • Plural ending in S: Add ' (The boys' shoes)
  • Irregular Plural: Add 's (The children's toys)

Yes/No Questions

Swap the subject and the verb be.

They are from Athens.
Are they from Athens?
Rule: Do NOT use contractions in affirmative short answers. (Yes, she is. NOT Yes, she's.)

3. Prepositions: In, On, At

IN

Place (Large Areas)
  • Cities (in Paris)
  • Countries (in France)
  • Rooms (in Room 215)
Time (Long Periods)
  • Years (in 2015)
  • Months (in May)
  • Seasons (in summer)
  • Times of day (in the morning)

ON

Place (Surfaces/Lines)
  • Streets (on Park St)
  • Floors (on the 2nd floor)
Time (Specific Days)
  • Dates (on June 29)
  • Days (on Sunday)
  • The weekend (on the weekend)

AT

Place (Specific Points)
  • Addresses (at 39 Main St)
  • Locations (at the store)
  • Exceptions: at school, at home, at work (No 'the')
Time (Exact Times)
  • Clock times (at 9:00)
  • at midnight / at noon
  • Exception: at night

4. Demonstratives (This, That, These, Those)

Used to point out specific people or things based on distance and quantity.

NEAR (Here)
Singular: THIS
This is my camera.
Plural: THESE
These are my sunglasses.
FAR (There)
Singular: THAT
That is a tall building.
Plural: THOSE
Are those your shoes?
