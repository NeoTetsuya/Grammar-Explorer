Mastering The Past

Learn to talk about completed actions, ongoing background events, irregular verbs, and sequence of time.

1. Simple Past of 'Be'

Was & Were

Was / Wasn't Use with I, He, She, It (Singular)
Were / Weren't Use with You, We, They (Plural)

Time Expressions

Last: night, week, year. (Never use "yesterday night" or "the last week")
Yesterday: morning, afternoon, evening.
Ago: Comes after the time (e.g., two weeks ago).

Questions

Was he there? Be + Subject
Who was on vacation? Who (Subject) = ALWAYS Was

2. Regular & Irregular Verbs

Action Completed in the Past

Spelling Rules (-ed)

General: +ed (packed) Silent e: +d (hiked) Consonant + y: -y → i + ed (studied) 1-syllable CVC: Double last letter (planned)*

*Don't double w, x, y (played). For 2-syllable CVC, only double if stress is on 2nd syllable (preferred), not 1st (visited).

Negative Statements

Always use didn't + BASE FORM. Do not use past tense after didn't.

They didn't take the bus. didn't + Base
They didn't took the bus.

Irregular Verb Groups

Group 1: buy→bought, go→went Group 2: eat→ate, win→won Group 3 (Unchanging): cost→cost, hit→hit

3. Who & What Questions

Subject vs Object Questions

Asking about the SUBJECT

Who/What performed the action?
Rule: Do NOT use 'did'. Use the Past Verb.

Who went with you? Who + Past Verb

Asking about the OBJECT

Who/What received the action?
Rule: Use 'did' + Base Verb.

Who did you ask? Who + did + Base Verb

4. Past Time Clauses

Begin with Before, After, or When. If the time clause is at the beginning, use a comma!

After they rested, Shackleton left. Comma when at start!
Shackleton left after they rested. (No comma)

5. Past Progressive

was/were + V-ing. Used for ongoing actions, or background actions interrupted by a Simple Past event.

I was walking when I heard a noise. Interrupted by Simple Past
Non-Action Verbs: Never use progressive for states/thoughts/senses (e.g., "knew", not "was knowing").
