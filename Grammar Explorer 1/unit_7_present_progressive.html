1. Statements & Spelling

Actions actively in progress right now.

The Formula

Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing
She is reading right now.
They are not (aren't) studying.

Spelling Rules (-ing)

Silent -e Drop -e, add -ing
make → making
-ie Ending Change to -y
die → dying
CVC (Consonant-Vowel-Consonant)
  • 1-syllable: Double last letter (sit → sitting)
  • 2-syllable (stress on 2nd): Double (begin → beginning)
  • 2-syllable (stress on 1st): Do NOT double (listen → listening)
  • Ends in w, x, y: Do NOT double (play → playing)

2. Forming Questions

Yes / No Questions

Move am/is/are before the subject.

Is Stephen taking a photo? Be + Subject + Verb-ing
No contractions in affirmative short answers:
Yes, he is. (NOT Yes, he's.)

Who: Subject vs Object

Asking about the Subject
Who is calling? Who + IS + Verb-ing (Always singular!)
Asking about the Object
Who are you studying with? Who + Be + Subject + Verb-ing

3. Non-Action Verbs Rule

Verbs that describe states, feelings, thoughts, possessions, or automatic senses are NOT physical actions. They cannot be used in the progressive (-ing) tense.

Emotions like, love, hate, want, need
Thoughts know, understand, remember
Possessions own, belong, have
Senses see, hear

Active vs. Automatic Senses

I see the train.
Automatic = No -ing. (Not "I am seeing")
I am watching a movie.
Chosen Action = -ing is OK.

Dual Meaning: "Have"

He has a car.
Meaning: Possession (No -ing).
He is having lunch.
Meaning: Activity/Eating (-ing is OK).
