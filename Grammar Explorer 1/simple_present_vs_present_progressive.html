Simple Present

Routines, habits, schedules, and facts.

1. Affirmative Rules

  • I, You, We, They: Use Base Form
    They work at a bank.
  • He, She, It: Add -s or -es
    He works at a bank.
  • Crucial: Never put "be" in front of another verb here.
    He is work → He works

2. Negative & Questions

Use auxiliary verbs do/does. The main verb stays in base form!

She doesn't exercise daily.
Does she walk a lot?

3. Time Clues

always, usually, sometimes, never, every day, once a week

Placed before verbs, but after "be".

Present Progressive

Actions happening right now, or around now.

1. The Formula

Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing
  • Affirmative:
    They are walking.
  • Negative:
    He is not studying.

2. Questions

Move the "be" verb to the front.

Are you listening?
Where are they going?

3. Time Clues

now, right now, at the moment, today, this week

The Non-Action Verb Exception

Verbs that describe feelings, thoughts, possessions, states, or automatic senses are NOT actions. Therefore, they cannot be used in the progressive (-ing) tense, even if it is happening right now.

The Rule in Action

I know the answer right now.
Use Simple Present for thoughts.
I am knowing the answer right now.

Dual Meanings (e.g., "Have")

Possession (Non-Action):
He has a car. (Not "is having")
Activity (Action):
He is having lunch. (Eating = Action)
