5.1 Present Perfect: Statements

Form: have / has + past participle
• I / You / We / They have planned (haven't planned)
• He / She / It has eaten (hasn't eaten)
Connecting past to present: Recently completed actions; indefinite past experiences; actions in unexpired time frames (*today, this week*).
Real English: When writing two present perfect verbs joined by and, do not repeat has or have (*Aasim has graduated from college and started a new job*).

Dr. Takanori Shibata with Paro, the robot seal p. 130

Factories have used robots to build cars and other machines for many years. Now, the science of robotics has reached a new level. Robots have now moved from factories into people’s homes.

Good Times and Bad Times for the Inuit

Arctic cultures confronting rapid environmental changes

CD2-10
Traveling by dog sled is an important part of Inuit culture. p. 144

The Inuit are the native people of the Arctic regions of Canada, Greenland, Russia, and the United States. In recent years, climate change has been making life difficult for the Inuit people of the Arctic. Their traditional culture has been under attack. Sea ice is very important in their lives. Recently, the sea ice has been melting. No sea ice means no seal hunting. No seal hunting means no meat for the dogs, and dogs are very important to the Inuit. These changes have caused social problems in Inuit communities everywhere.

In some countries, however, life for Inuit people has been improving. In Alaska, many Inuit have been earning money from shares¹ in local companies. In Canada, changes to the law have given the Inuit more independence from the national government. They live in regions that earn money from mining and pipelines.² Russia, however, has no special areas or programs for its Inuit people.

For the Inuit, Greenland is special. Around 85 percent of the island’s people are Inuit. The Inuit have been driving dog sleds across the icy land for over 700 years. Denmark used to govern Greenland. However, since 2008, the Inuit people of Greenland have been running their own country.

¹ share: an equal part of the ownership of a company

² pipeline: a system of connected pipes used to carry gas, water, or oil over long distances

The sun went down three hours ago. Since then, it’s gotten very dark. The moon has risen, and the stars have been appearing in the sky.

Many birds, butterflies, and small animals have disappeared for the night. Others have become active. A raccoon is in my yard. It’s been coming into my yard every night this winter. I hear an owl. I’ve heard it a few times before.

Since the sun went down, it’s become much colder. The temperature has already dropped six degrees. I’ve put on a warm sweater, socks, and slippers. I like a lot of things about winter, but not the cold!

GRAMMAR FOCUS (Page 154)

• The writer uses the present perfect to describe recently completed actions: It has gotten very dark. The temperature has already dropped six degrees.

• The writer uses the present perfect progressive to talk about actions that started in the past and continue to the present: It has been coming into my yard every night this winter.
