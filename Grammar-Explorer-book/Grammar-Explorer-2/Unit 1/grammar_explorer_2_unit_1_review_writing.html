Error Breakdown:

• always is starting: Frequency adverbs come before regular verbs (always starts).

• last: Singular subject it takes lasts.

• gets: Plural subject thousands... and visitors takes base verb get.

• is using: Annual habitual fact takes simple present uses.

• start: Action in progress around the present time takes are starting.

WRITING FOCUS: Capitalizing Proper Nouns (Page 23)

Proper nouns name specific people, places, and things. Always capitalize:

  • Streets, cities, states, countries, continents: Paseo de la Reforma, Venice, Florida, Canada, Africa
  • Days of week, months, holidays: Sunday, February, Independence Day
  • People and special events: Professor Ortega, Kila Raipur Sports Festival
