[169] P: An Afar Wedding
[170] P: What is an Afar wedding ceremony like?
[171] P: The actual wedding ceremony is only for men. The bride1 does not go to this ceremony. Instead, her father represents2 her. The women celebrate3 in a different place. They sit and talk about the bride’s life.
[172] P: The women’s ceremony often includes a lot of dancing.
[173] P: What happens next?
[174] P: After these separate ceremonies, the bride and groom4 meet with their families and friends. Sometimes this party lasts all night. The next day, the bride and groom invite their families to their new home.
[175] P: They eat a traditional5 meal. A typical meal is goat with rice.
[176] P: What do the bride and groom wear?
[177] P: Often they both dress in traditional clothes, but the bride sometimes wears a western-style dress. Some brides wear a lot of gold jewelry.
[178] P: Who pays for the wedding?
[179] P: In the Afar culture, the man pays for the wedding. He also provides a home for his new wife, but they do not live there together until three days after the wedding.

[186] P: 5 traditional: based on things that people have done in a certain way for a long time
