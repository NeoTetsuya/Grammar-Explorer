Past Tenses

Master the **Simple Past** (completed actions) and the **Past Progressive** (actions in progress). Watch out for irregular verbs!

1. Simple Past of 'Be'

Was / Were

The Forms

WAS / WASN'T I, He, She, It, Singular
WERE / WEREN'T We, You, They, Plural

Interactive Examples

She was at home.
They weren't happy.

Time Clues

  • last: night, week, year (No 'the')
  • yesterday: morning, afternoon (No 'yesterday night')
  • ago: Place after the time (e.g., two days ago)

2. Regular & Irregular Verbs

Completed Actions

Affirmative Rules

Same form for ALL subjects.

  • Regular: Add -ed or -d (e.g., walked, hiked).
  • Double Consonant: CVC verbs double the last letter if stressed on the final syllable (e.g., plan → planned, prefer → preferred). But NOT if ending in w, x, y.
  • Irregular: Memorize groups (e.g., go → went, put → put).
CRITICAL

Negative Statements (didn't)

Always use didn't + BASE FORM. Do not use the past tense verb after didn't.

They didn't take the bus.
They didn't took the bus.

3. Questions (Subject vs Object)

When using Who or What, the structure changes depending on what you are asking about.

Asking about the SUBJECT

Do NOT use 'did'. Use Past Verb.

Who went with you?

Asking about the OBJECT

Use 'did' + Base Verb.

Who did you ask?

4. Past Progressive

Actions in progress in the past.

Subject + was/were + Verb-ing

Interrupted Actions

Background ongoing action (Progressive) is interrupted by a sudden completed action (Simple Past).

I was walking when I heard a noise.
