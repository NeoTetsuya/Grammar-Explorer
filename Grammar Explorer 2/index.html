Grammar Explorer 2 Quick Reference

Present Perfect vs Simple Past

Use Present Perfect (has/have + V3) for unspecified time or ongoing life experiences (e.g. I have visited Japan). Use Simple Past for specific finished times (e.g. I visited Japan in 2022).

Gerunds vs Infinitives

Certain verbs require a gerund (enjoy learning, avoid making), while others require an infinitive (decide to study, hope to win). Prepositions always take gerunds.

Passive Voice Basics

Form passive with Be + Past Participle. Use when the receiver of the action is the focal point or the agent is unknown/unimportant: The museum was built in 1920.
